Florida Basketball Jerseys — Refund Policy
Last updated: January 1, 2025
Effective: January 1, 2025

Because every product we make is custom-designed and produced specifically for your team, our refund policy differs from standard retail returns. Please read this policy carefully before placing an order. By submitting a quote request and approving a design proof, you agree to the terms outlined below.

1
Section 1

Our Core Commitment

We stand behind everything we produce. If we make a mistake — a production error, a defect in materials, or a jersey that does not match your approved proof — we will make it right at no cost to you. This is our fundamental promise to every customer.

We invest in a design proof approval process specifically to ensure that what you approve is exactly what gets produced. When both parties confirm the design in writing, we hold ourselves accountable to delivering that exact product.

Our promise: If we produce something that does not match your approved proof due to our error, we will reprint the affected jerseys or issue a full or partial refund — whichever resolves the issue most appropriately for you.

2
Section 2

What Is & Isn't Eligible for a Refund

The following scenarios outline when a refund, reprint, or remedy is — and is not — applicable:

Eligible
Production Error by Us

The delivered jerseys contain errors that differ from your approved design proof — wrong colors, wrong names, wrong numbers, wrong logo placement, or incorrect sizing compared to what was ordered and approved.

Eligible
Defective Materials or Manufacturing

Jerseys arrive with material defects, printing defects, stitching failures, or quality issues not caused by normal wear and use. This includes premature cracking, peeling, or fading that occurs before the item has been used.

Eligible
Lost or Significantly Damaged in Shipping

Your order is confirmed lost by the carrier or arrives with significant damage that occurred during transit and is documented with carrier evidence.

Not Eligible
Errors Present in the Approved Proof

If a misspelled name, wrong number, incorrect color, or any other error was present in the design proof you approved, the production is correct and a refund or reprint cannot be issued. Review all proofs carefully before approving.

Not Eligible
Change of Mind or Team Roster Changes

Refunds are not available because a player left the team, the season was cancelled, you changed your color preference after production began, or for any reason unrelated to a defect or production error.

Not Eligible
Sizing Issues Based on Customer-Provided Information

If you submitted sizing information in your order and the jerseys match those exact sizes, we cannot accept returns due to fit preferences or sizing choices made at the time of ordering. We provide size guidance upon request.

Not Eligible
Damage Caused by Washing, Wear, or Misuse

Damage caused by improper washing, machine drying at high heat, bleaching, or general misuse after delivery is not covered under our refund policy.

Case by Case
Partial Orders with Mixed Issues

If part of a bulk order has production issues and part does not, we will assess each affected item individually and offer a targeted remedy — reprint of affected items or partial credit — rather than a full order refund.

3
Section 3

How to Submit a Refund or Reprint Request

If you believe your order qualifies for a refund or reprint based on the criteria above, please follow these steps:

1
Contact Us Within 7 Days of Delivery

All claims must be submitted within 7 calendar days of confirmed delivery. Contact us at [email protected] or call (407) 555-0192.

2
Provide Your Order Details

Include your order reference number, the name on the order, and the delivery date so we can locate your order quickly.

3
Send Clear Photos of the Issue

Attach clear photographs showing the defect or discrepancy. Photos should show the full jersey and a close-up of the specific issue. This allows us to assess the claim accurately and quickly.

4
We Review and Respond Within 3 Business Days

Our team will review your claim and photographic evidence against your approved proof. We will respond within 3 business days with our assessment and proposed remedy.

5
Remedy Is Applied

If your claim is approved, we will arrange either a reprint of the affected jerseys at no charge or issue a refund to your original payment method. Reprints are typically the preferred remedy as they get your team the jerseys they need.

Do not return items before contacting us. We will advise whether items need to be returned as part of the claims process. Unsolicited returns will not be accepted and may delay your claim.

4
Section 4

Refund Methods & Timelines

Where a refund is approved, we will process it using the following methods and timelines:

  • Original Payment Method: Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the order.
  • Processing Time: Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days. The time for funds to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider and may take an additional 3–7 business days.
  • Partial Refunds: Where only part of an order is affected, a partial refund proportional to the affected items will be issued.
  • Store Credit Option: In some cases, we may offer store credit as an alternative to a cash refund, at your discretion. Store credit has no expiry date.
  • Reprint Option: In most cases involving production errors, a reprint is the fastest and most practical remedy. We will always offer this as an option.

Shipping costs on the original order are non-refundable in cases where only part of the order is affected. If the entire order is affected by a production error on our part, all associated costs including shipping are refundable.

5
Section 5

Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled or modified only under the following conditions:

  • Before proof approval: Orders may be cancelled at any time before you have approved a design proof. No charges will be applied at this stage.
  • After proof approval but before payment: If you have approved a proof but not yet paid, you may cancel without penalty. Your approved proof will be held on file for 30 days should you wish to proceed later.
  • After proof approval and payment: Once payment has been received and production has begun, orders cannot be cancelled or modified. Production of custom items begins immediately upon payment confirmation.
  • Mid-production changes: Changes to names, numbers, colors, or sizing cannot be made once production has started. Any changes at this stage would require a new order.

If you are unsure about any details of your order, do not approve your proof until you are fully satisfied. We encourage you to share the proof with your team, coaching staff, or players before giving final approval. There is no time limit on your proof review — take the time you need.

6
Section 6

Proof Approval Responsibility

The design proof approval stage is the most important step in the entire ordering process. When you approve a proof in writing, you are confirming that:

  • All player names are spelled correctly
  • All player numbers are correct
  • Colors match your team's requirements
  • Logo placement and sizing is correct
  • The overall design layout is as you expected
  • The size run (quantities per size) is accurate

We recommend reviewing the proof against your original roster list and checking each player's details individually before approving. We offer unlimited revisions before approval — use them.

7
Section 7

Shipping-Related Issues

Once your order has been dispatched and tracking information has been provided, responsibility for delivery passes to the shipping carrier. However, we will always assist you in resolving any shipping issues.

  • Lost Orders: If your order is confirmed lost by the carrier, we will work with the carrier to file a claim and arrange a reprint or refund once the investigation is complete.
  • Damaged in Transit: If your order arrives damaged due to shipping, document the damage with photos and contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will work with you to resolve the issue.
  • Incorrect Address: If an order is undeliverable or returned due to an incorrect address provided by you, re-shipping costs will be your responsibility. Please double-check your delivery address before order confirmation.
  • Delivery Delays: We are not responsible for carrier delays. If a deadline is critical to your order, please communicate this clearly at the time of quoting so we can plan accordingly.
8
