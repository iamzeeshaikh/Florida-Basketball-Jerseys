Buying Guidance

Not Sure Which
Style to Order?

Every team has different needs. Game jerseys, practice jerseys, reversible uniforms, youth sizes, bulk orders — it can be a lot to sort through. Use this guide to find the right fit for your specific situation, then request a free quote with confidence.

Game Day Uniforms

You need official jerseys for competitive games

You want something that looks sharp, represents your team's brand, and holds up through a full season of play. Sublimated jerseys are the strongest choice here — full-color, edge-to-edge design that won't crack or peel.

Best fit Sublimated Jerseys
Home & Away

You need both a home and away jersey without double the cost

Reversible jerseys are built for exactly this. Two different designs — one on each side. Your players flip the jersey depending on which team is wearing which color. Perfect for travel teams and leagues.

Best fit Reversible Jerseys
Youth Programs

You're outfitting kids — ages 6 through 18

Youth sizes run from XS to XL and can be mixed with adult sizes in a single order. Lightweight and mesh fabrics work best for younger players — breathable, easy to move in, and durable enough for weekly games.

Best fit Youth Uniforms
Practice & Training

You need jerseys for daily practice — not just game day

Practice jerseys take more punishment than game jerseys. You want something durable, comfortable, and easy to wash. Mesh practice jerseys are the go-to — affordable enough to outfit the whole squad including alternates.

Best fit Practice Jerseys
Full Uniform Set

You want a complete matched uniform — jersey and shorts together

A matching jersey and shorts set creates a professional, cohesive look on the court. We custom-produce the shorts to match your jersey colors and design exactly — ordered as a set from a single quote.

Best fit Full Uniform Sets
Women's Programs

You're ordering for a women's or girls' basketball team

Women's cut jerseys are tailored for a better athletic fit. Available in all the same custom options — names, numbers, logos, colors — in women's XS through 3XL and girls' youth sizing.

Best fit Women's Uniforms
