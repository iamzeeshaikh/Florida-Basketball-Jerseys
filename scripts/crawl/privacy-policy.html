3
Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Order Fulfilment: To process your quote request, create your design proof, produce your jerseys, and arrange delivery to your address.
  • Communication: To respond to your enquiries, send design proofs, provide order updates, and follow up on delivery.
  • Customer Support: To assist you with questions, issues, or requests relating to your order.
  • Re-Orders: To store your design on file for future re-orders, allowing us to reproduce matching jerseys for new players.
  • Business Operations: To maintain our records, process payments, and manage our business legally and efficiently.
  • Website Improvement: To understand how visitors use our website and make improvements to our services.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations.
  • Marketing (with your consent): To send you occasional information about our services and promotions, only if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to serving your order and maintaining our business.

4
Section 4

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Shipping Carriers: We share your name and delivery address with shipping carriers (such as UPS, FedEx, or USPS) solely to deliver your order.
  • Payment Processors: Payment information is processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store payment card details on our servers.
  • Production Partners: In some cases, we may work with trusted production partners who assist in manufacturing your jerseys. These partners are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your information for any other purpose.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Florida Basketball Jerseys, our customers, or others.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance of any such change.

All third parties who receive your data are required to handle it securely and only for the specific purpose for which it was shared. No third party has permission to use your data for their own marketing or business purposes.

5
Section 5

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our site. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your browser.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality such as form submission and page navigation. These cannot be disabled without affecting the website.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., which pages are most visited). We may use tools like Google Analytics for this purpose.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for a better experience on return visits.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to display relevant advertisements. These are only placed with your consent.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For more information about how we use cookies specifically, please see our Cookie Policy.

6
Section 6

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including for the duration of our business relationship with you and as required by applicable law.

  • Order records and correspondence are retained for a minimum of 7 years for legal and accounting purposes.
  • Design files are stored on file to support re-orders. You may request deletion of your design files at any time by contacting us.
  • Website analytics data is retained in aggregate, anonymized form for business analysis.
  • Marketing communications subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

When your data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize it securely.

7
Section 7

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include:

  • SSL encryption on our website to protect data transmitted between your browser and our server.
  • Secure email communications for sharing sensitive order information.
  • Restricted access to personal data — only authorized team members who need it to fulfil your order can access it.
  • Secure storage of order records and design files.
  • Use of trusted, reputable third-party processors for payment handling.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. In the event of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at adults, including coaches, athletic directors, parents, and program administrators. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

When an order includes custom jerseys for youth players (such as a youth basketball team), the personal information submitted (player names, numbers, sizes) is provided by an adult on behalf of the team or program, not directly by the child.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at [email protected] and we will take prompt action to delete it.
