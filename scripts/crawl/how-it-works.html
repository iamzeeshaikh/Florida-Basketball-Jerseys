The Full Process

Every Step Explained

Here is exactly what happens from the moment you submit a quote request to the day your team gets their jerseys.

1 Step
Step 1 — You Do This
Submit Your Free Quote Request
<2 min
To complete

Fill in our free quote form with the basics of your order. You don't need a finished design, a logo, or any specific files to get started — just tell us what you're looking for and we'll take it from there.

The form takes less than 2 minutes. No payment is required at this stage. No account needed. No commitment of any kind.

If you're not sure about specific details — jersey style, exact quantities, or design preferences — that's completely fine. We'll work through those questions with you during the process.

What to include in your request
Jersey style — game, practice, reversible, etc.
Approximate quantity needed
Team name and colors you have in mind
Any design ideas or reference images
Your delivery deadline if you have one
Your logo — if you have one (not required)
Submit Your Free Quote
2 Step
Step 2 — We Do This
We Build Your Free Design Proof
24–48h
Turnaround

After reviewing your request, our design team gets to work creating a full digital mockup of your jerseys — completely free, no charge. We show the front and back of the jersey with your colors, logo, team name, and any other design elements you've requested.

If you don't have a logo, we'll create one based on your team name and preferences. If you have reference images or a rough idea, we'll use those as inspiration.

No payment is required at this stage. The proof is provided purely so you can see exactly how your jerseys will look before committing to anything.

What your proof includes
Full-color front and back jersey mockup
Your exact colors matched to your request
Your team name and logo positioned on the jersey
Example player name and number layout
Itemised pricing breakdown for your order
Confirmed production and delivery timeline
3 Step
Step 3 — You Then We
You Approve — We Produce
7–14
Days production

Review the proof carefully. Share it with your players, coaching staff, or athletic director. Request as many changes as you need — there is no limit and no charge for revisions. We revise until you're completely happy.

Once you approve the proof in writing and payment is received, your order goes straight into production. We recommend reviewing every detail carefully at this stage: player names, numbers, colors, logo position, and sizing.

Nothing goes to press until you confirm. Once production begins, changes cannot be made — so take the time you need on the proof review.

Before you approve — check these
Every player name is spelled correctly
All player numbers are correct
Colors match your team requirements
Logo placement and size is correct
Size run quantities are accurate
Overall design layout is as expected
4 Done
Step 4 — Game Ready
Delivered Free to Your Door
3–7
Days delivery

Once production is complete, every jersey is individually inspected against your approved proof — colors verified, names and numbers checked, sizes confirmed. Only when everything is correct does your order get packed and dispatched.

Your jerseys are shipped free to any US address. We'll send you a tracking number as soon as your order is dispatched so you can follow it in real time.

Bulk orders going to a single address ship together. Jerseys are packed and labelled by player where possible to make distribution day straightforward.

What happens before dispatch
Every jersey checked against approved proof
Colors verified for consistency across the set
Names and numbers checked against roster
Order packed and labelled by size/player
Tracking number sent to you on dispatch
Free shipping to any US address
