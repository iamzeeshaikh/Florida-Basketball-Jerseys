Full-Dye Custom Jerseys for Florida Teams

A sublimated basketball jersey is a fully custom, full-color jersey where the design is permanently fused into the fabric — not printed on top of it. Using a heat-transfer process called dye sublimation, your colors, logos, names, and numbers become part of the fabric itself, producing vivid, edge-to-edge artwork that will not crack, peel, or fade.

This makes sublimated jerseys the highest-quality, longest-lasting option for Florida basketball teams that want a professional look without a professional budget. Every jersey in your order is produced to exactly the same specification — consistent colors, consistent quality, across your entire roster.

Because the design starts from a blank canvas, there are no template restrictions. Your colors, your logo, your player names, your numbers — every element is built around your team's identity from scratch.

Flatlock Stitching

All seams are finished with flatlock stitching — a technique that lays the seam flat against the fabric rather than bunching it. This eliminates chafing at underarm and side seams during intense play.

Reinforced Stress Points

High-stress areas — collar, armhole, and side seams — are reinforced with bar-tack stitching at key stress points, preventing seam separation during physical play and extended wear.

Hemmed Armholes

Armhole edges are clean-hemmed with a rolled finish to prevent fraying and maintain a professional appearance across the full lifespan of the jersey — season after season.

Pre-Shrunk Construction

All fabric is pre-shrunk before cutting and production, ensuring the jersey you receive maintains its shape and size after washing. The size you order is the size you get — even after repeated machine washes.

Care Instructions — Keep Your Jerseys Looking New
Machine wash cold Use cold water on a gentle or delicate cycle to preserve color vibrancy.
Wash inside out Turning the jersey inside out before washing protects the printed surface.
Hang to air dry Air drying preserves fabric shape and extends the life of the sublimation print.
No high-heat drying High heat in tumble dryers can warp the fabric and degrade the print over time.
No bleach or softeners Bleach and fabric softeners break down the polyester fibres and degrade color over time.
Do not iron the print Never iron directly on the printed surface — heat will damage the dye sublimation permanently.

Florida teams — outdoor and summer courts: For jerseys worn on outdoor courts in Florida's sun and heat, our UPF 30+ polyester fabric provides additional protection while the moisture-wicking surface keeps players cooler during long outdoor sessions. Air drying is especially effective in Florida's warm climate — jerseys dry in minutes.

How to Measure — Jersey
1
Chest: Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape horizontal and under the armpits.
2
Waist: Measure around the natural waistline — the narrowest part of the torso, usually 1 inch above the belly button.
3
Hip: Measure around the fullest part of the hips and seat, keeping the tape level all the way around.
4
Height: Measure from the top of the head to the floor in bare feet. Use a wall and a book for accuracy.
How to Measure — Shorts
1
Waist: Measure around the natural waistline where the shorts will sit — not the hips. Keep the tape snug but not tight.
2
Hip: Measure around the fullest part of the seat and hips. This is the widest measurement when standing straight.
3
Inseam: Measure from the crotch seam down to the desired hem length on the inner thigh. Standard basketball shorts fall mid-thigh.
4
When in doubt: Size up for shorts. A slightly looser fit is always more comfortable during physical play than a fit that is too snug.
When between sizes — size up

Basketball is an active sport. A jersey that is slightly larger allows full range of motion without restriction. If a player falls between two sizes, always select the larger size.

Youth sizes can overlap adult Small

Youth XL and adult Small are very close in measurement. For players aged 13–15, we recommend measuring carefully and comparing against both charts before deciding.
